What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small pieces of data stored on your computer or device via your browser by sites you visit. As is common practice with almost all websites, this site uses cookies to improve your experience by remembering your preferences and enabling other cookie-based features (e.g. analytics), either for a single visit (through a "session cookie") or for multiple repeat visits (using a "persistent cookie").

Our Cookies

We use cookies for a number of different purposes, including keeping the site working correctly and understanding how visitors use it.

Third-Party Cookies

In special cases, we also use cookies provided by trusted third parties like Google Analytics. Third-party analytics are used to track and measure usage of this site so that we can continue to produce engaging content.

Managing Cookies

When you first access the site, you may receive a message advising you that cookies and similar technologies are in use. By clicking "Accept", you signify that you understand and agree to the use of these technologies, as described in this Cookie Policy.

You may withdraw consent at any time. Most browsers allow you to refuse to accept cookies, and you can remove cookies from your browser settings. Be aware that disabling cookies may affect the functionality of this and many other websites you visit.
